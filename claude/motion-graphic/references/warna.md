# Colour: palette and colour gates

Opened when locking the palette (Baca bahan to Dunia visual steps of the `SKILL.md` workflow) and before handing frames over. Colour words in a brief ("soft blue") are not values; a palette is measured from a reference or the brand profile. Contrast of text: `teks.md` (≥ 4.5 : 1).

## Where the palette comes from
Lock it before any design, in this order: the user's references (REF-SPEC) and brief; the brand profile (e.g. Jadisatu → `jadisatu-gelap`/`jadisatu-terang`); for client or third-party footage, the footage itself (one dominant scene colour + one accent); otherwise an example from `palettes.json` (10 palettes measured from approved references) whose mood fits: studio-merah (firm talking head, dark studio), hitam-kuning-hangat (personal, reflective), merah-menyala (dramatic education, mystery), merah-kertas-dokumenter (archive photos), putih-lavender (light, friendly product), biru-gradasi-terang (clean finance/product), hitam-navy (serious finance, night), monokrom (minimal; emphasis through light). Reason: without a locked palette every graphic picks its own colour and the result is a rainbow.

## Using it
- **One dominant accent family**: the largest family ≥ 70 % of coloured pixels, at most 2 families; the second only from footage, lights, or a third-party logo. Every measured reference had a dominant family of 74–100 %.
- **Neutrals carry the frame, the accent marks**: black, white and grey fill most of the screen, accent usually ≤ 15 % of area, except a full-screen accent world (colour card or panel) designed as a pause.
- **Derivatives follow the accent**: tints and contact shadows use the accent hue (≤ 25° from it), no new colour. Gradients and glow are fine within one hue family and with a reason (NEGATIF 2); a gradient commits (a crisp dense zone, following the story's acts, or as in REF-SPEC), never a random radial smudge or one hesitant tone. No dark vignette on a light background, no brown + black base.
- On dark backgrounds a deep accent (pure blue on navy, ±3.6 : 1) fails; use its light version or white.
- Third-party assets (logo, real product UI) may sit outside the palette if small and never used as the accent.

## Gates
`scripts/warna/cek-warna.mjs` (Node; `npm ci` once in `scripts/warna/`; thresholds and calibration in `scripts/warna/README.md`).
- **Entry** (before designing): `node scripts/warna/cek-warna.mjs masuk <video|still folder|png...> [--ke-spec REF-SPEC.json] [--ke-tema tema.ts]` writes `palet.json`: per frame type (light/dark) 4-5 real HEX values with area %, role (background = largest area, ink = highest contrast to background, accent = most saturated OKLCH, neutral/surface), luminance, WCAG ratio, and ink:background APCA Lc. `--ke-spec` adds the `palet` key; `--ke-tema` writes `tema.ts` tokens (derived values tagged). Colours in code come from these tokens, not guesses.
- **Exit** (each frame before handoff): `node scripts/warna/cek-warna.mjs keluar <frame.png|folder> --palet palet.json [--html frame.html | --tanpa-teks noteks.png] [--abaikan x,y,w,h]` reports three things: **warna-nyasar**, colour clusters ≥ 0.5 % area with ΔE2000 > 12 to every palette colour (tints/shades ±25 L and palette mixes included; footage excluded with `--abaikan`); **kontras-apca**, per text block APCA Lc (the tool marks < 60 body, < 45 large ≥ 48 px or bold ≥ 38 px; WCAG 2 as a notice), text separated by rendering with and without it (HTML `data-lapis="teks"`, Remotion a second `sembunyikanTeks` still from `toolkit/starter/src/Video.tsx`; axe-core as a notice for HTML); **warna-nanggung**, areas ≥ 3 % in the muddy band (OKLCH chroma 0.03-0.08 at lightness 0.45-0.75) that are not palette colours: pick a neutral (chroma < 0.03) or a decisive colour (≥ 0.08), or add it from the reference if it truly belongs.
- Text that fades as story language gets `data-sengaja="pudar"`; blurred (≥ 1.5 px) and occluded text are skipped (logged in `dilewati`). `gerbang_produksi.sh` and `board.py` run the exit gate when `palet.json` exists.
- `python3 scripts/palette_check.py render.mp4` flags > 2 families or a dominant family < 70 %; skin and footage lights count (hue 0–60°), so read it with a still on talking heads.
