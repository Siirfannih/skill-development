## Honest differentiation angles
1. **Not a POS app**: no need to learn a POS system; just send an ordinary WhatsApp chat message. Fits people selling without a storefront/cashier desk (consignment sellers, online sellers, just starting out).
2. **One place for scattered data**: from POS to marketplaces, summarized together; per branch.
3. **Stock is calculated, not only logged**: forecast + suggested purchase quantities.
