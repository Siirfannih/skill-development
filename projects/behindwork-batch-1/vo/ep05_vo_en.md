# Ep 05 · VO story version, English (D-15)

Same facts and beats as `ep05_vo_tts.md` (Indonesian story version, D-14; facts from `riset/episode/05-slack-game-gagal/script.md` v2), rewritten in natural spoken English for Indonesian listeners: simple, common words, flowing sentences joined with commas, short and long alternating, calm "but" for the turn, no sudden one-word rebuttal. `...` for a breath or to delay one key word; CAPS at most one word per beat. Numbers are written as spoken words; acronyms and names in their official spelling. Target tempo 2.4–2.7 words/sec (D-16). The Indonesian subtitle column is static at the bottom of the screen (D-15): `<br>` = line break (max 42 characters per line), ` / ` = next subtitle card (max 2 lines each). Beat number = row order of the script table. The parallel pairs (03/04, 05/06, 10/11, 13/14) keep the same sentence pattern and punctuation so the prosody matches (Gagal Dua Kali structure). Beat 08 quotes Tiny Speck's announcement in its original English ("we only managed to create a crazy, beautiful game").
