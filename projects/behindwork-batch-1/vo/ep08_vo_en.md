# Ep 08 · VO story version, English (D-15)

Same facts and beats as `ep08_vo_tts.md` (Indonesian story version, D-14; facts from `riset/episode/08-dyson-5127-prototipe/script.md`), rewritten in natural spoken English for Indonesian listeners: simple, common words, flowing sentences joined with commas, short and long alternating, calm "but" for the turn, no sudden one-word rebuttal. `...` for a breath or to delay one key word; CAPS at most one word per beat. Numbers are written as spoken words; acronyms and names in their official spelling. Target tempo 2.4–2.7 words/sec (D-16). The Indonesian subtitle column is static at the bottom of the screen (D-15): `<br>` = line break (max 42 characters per line), ` / ` = next subtitle card (max 2 lines each). The count stays the backbone of the story (Penghitung structure): the failure montage in beat 06 is read as one sentence that speeds up, not as separate stops. Beat number = row order of the script table. The soft CAPS on SIX/SEVEN in beats 08–09 keep the myth correction (5,126 failed, number 5,127 worked). Dyson's own 2-sec audio ("the 5,127 worked") is optional on the visuals of beat 09; the VO is still read.
